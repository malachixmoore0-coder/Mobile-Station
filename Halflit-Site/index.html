Brand identity

The hour
between.

HALFLIT is named after the streetlight hour — when the day's done but the night hasn't decided what it is yet. It's the same place most of us are at twenty: not a kid, not quite grown, working it out in real time.

We make heavyweight basics and loud-but-considered graphics for that in-between. Every drop is named for a phase of the moon and carries its own version of the mark, so a piece from Drop 01 never looks quite like a piece from Drop 03.
