# Gridiron AI — identity directions (round two)

Ten brand identities for **Gridiron AI** (NFL) and **CFB Gridiron AI** (college).
Each has a drawn logo, an app icon, a repeating pattern, a palette and a type pairing.

Open `identity.html` for the board — every mark is inline SVG, so it can be lifted
straight out of the file.

Round one (`concepts.html`, `README.md`) proposed screen treatments. Half of it
borrowed from software rather than football: terminal monospace, neon on black, a node
graph, the glow. This set is drawn from football's own material culture instead —
heraldry, chains, film stock, card stock, leather, brass, ticket paper, survey maps,
helmet decals and wire copy.

Marks are concepts, not final artwork. A chosen one should be redrawn on a proper grid
with optical corrections and a full size ramp.

## 11 — The Crest

Names: **Meridian**, **The Association**, **Gridiron Athletic**

An athletic association, not a product. Inscriptional caps, a real shield, and gold used
the way an institution uses gold.

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Bottle | `#0A2A20` |
| Field | `#0E3B2E` |
| Gold | `#C9A227` |
| Parchment | `#F0EAD8` |
| Oxblood | `#7B2D26` |

**Logo** A shield with a football above a gold band, two chevrons below.
**Pattern** Heraldic diaper — a diagonal lattice with a gold point at every crossing.
**Type** Cinzel 500/700/900 · Spectral 300/400/700
**Signature** Each of the 130 programmes gets a generated crest in its own colours, built
on this shield.

## 19 — The Stripe

Names: **Stripe**, **Wing**, **Sticker**

The strongest graphic device the sport owns. One stripe over the crown of a helmet
identifies a programme from the back row of a stadium, and it scales from a decal to a
stadium banner without changing.

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Jet | `#0C0C0D` |
| Shell | `#F2F1EE` |
| Stripe | `#D01B2B` |
| Cage | `#8A9199` |

**Logo** A helmet seen head-on with a red centre stripe and two facemask bars.
**Pattern** Stripes at four widths, never evenly spaced; the widths encode the four matrix

**Type** Anton · Archivo 400/500/700
**Signature** Each team gets a generated helmet in its real shell and stripe colours, used

the opposite of a terminal.

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Newsprint | `#D6D2C7` |
| Copy | `#EDEBE2` |
| Ink | `#1A1A18` |
| Press | `#2A4C6D` |

**Logo** A circular wire-service bug with a tick ring, a football and a red rule.

## Which mark survives at 40 pixels

| # | Identity | Rooted in | Best for | Mark at small size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11 | The Crest | Athletic heraldry | Both | Strong. Shield silhouettes are the most legible shape in the set. |
| 12 | Chain Gang | The chain crew | Both | Good. The three links read even when the stakes blur out. |
| 13 | Autumn Reel | 16mm film | Pro | Fair. Sprocket rhythm holds; the lens outline thins out fast. |
| 14 | Series One | Trading cards | Both | Fair. The border survives; the interior needs simplifying below 32px. |
| 15 | Pigskin | The ball | Both | Excellent. Laces alone are unmistakable at any size. |
| 16 | Drumline | Marching band | College | Strong. Radial lugs give it a distinctive outer edge. |
| 17 | Admit One | Ticket stock | College | Good. Unusual silhouette; the perforation needs to coarsen when small. |
| 18 | Contour | Survey maps | Both | Good. Drop to three rings below 32px and it stays clean. |
| 19 | The Stripe | Helmet decals | Both | Excellent. Highest contrast and the simplest silhouette here. |
| 20 | The Wire | Wire service | Pro | Good. The tick ring is the tell; it can drop to twelve ticks when small. |

## Recommendation

**Pigskin**, **Contour**, and **The Stripe**.

- **Pigskin** has the best mark of the ten and the warmest palette, and the laces solve
  the icon problem outright.
- **Contour** is the only direction whose pattern and whose charts are the same drawing,
  so the identity does real work instead of decorating.
- **The Stripe** is the one that would still look right in five years, and its per-team
  helmets give you 130 identities for the price of one.

If the two apps split: Drumline or Admit One for college, The Wire or Autumn Reel for pro.
